Proper BoltStack

Get your propers.

Every time you pull off something worth respect, you earn a colored washer. The rider who saw you do it stamps it, and it goes on a bolt on your cage. You can't buy your way onto it. You earn it first, then you pick it up at the BoltStack table. Over time, your stack tells people what kind of rider you are without you having to say a word.

How it works

One washer, one proper

Each accomplishment gets you one washer. The color tells people which vibe it came from.

The stamper

Someone who already has a stack has to see it happen or check the proof. Then they stamp the date and their initials into the washer. If there's no stamp, it doesn't count.

The Sprout comes first

Your first washer comes from whoever brought you out to your first lot day or group ride. Everybody can tell you who gave them theirs.

Capstones

Five of the vibes have one big accomplishment that gets a larger washer, so it stands out in the stack.

Stunts stay on the lot

Stunt washers only get stamped at lot days or closed venues. Nobody's earning one on the street.

Earn it, then get it proper

You can't buy a washer you haven't earned. Once a stamper signs off, you pay a few bucks at the BoltStack table for the engraved washer and the stamping. After that it's yours for good.

Getting it proper

  1. Pull it off. Do the thing in front of someone who already has a stack, or bring them the proof.
  2. Get the nod. The stamper signs off.
  3. Hit the table. Come find the BoltStack table and pay for your washer.
  4. Get stamped proper. The stamper strikes the date and their initials into it, right there.
  5. Stack it. Thread it on next to your last one.

Flair

Not everything on a stack has to be earned. I 3D print flair that rides right next to your propers: lot day tokens that prove you were there, crew tokens, dividers between your colors, and end caps. Those you can just buy.

The rule is simple: metal is earned, plastic is flair. A proper is always a round metal washer, and flair never is, so nobody mixes them up. I'm working on the first batch now.

Season drops and limited editions

Every riding season, a handful of new accomplishments drop and get added to the list, numbered 101 and up. Lot days and big ride outs get their own limited-run flair, printed for that event only. If you weren't there, you can't get it.

Lot days

The table goes wherever the riders are. I'll be at as many lot days as I can get to, and I'm hosting some too. That's where you get your propers, meet people, and see what everybody's been up to.
